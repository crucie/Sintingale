## Product decisions recorded during the build

- **"Tickets"** confirmed as each member's **contribution** to a project. The owner enters contribution points per member; shares are derived in basis points with the largest-remainder method, always totalling exactly 10,000 bps.
- **Teams & ENS is an opt-in feature**, off by default and switched per merchant in Dashboard → Settings. While off, team pages and APIs are disabled, project invoices are hidden, and checkout makes no ENS lookups.
- **Org-scoped entities** (`org`, `project`, `member`, `invite`, `grant`, `revocation`) are written by the relayer key but carry an EIP-191 signature from the org owner over their exact attributes. `isValidOrgEntity()` accepts an entity only if `$creator` is the owner, or `$creator` is the relayer **and** the owner signature verifies. This keeps merchants off a third network (no Arkiv gas for them) while staying verifiable by anyone.
